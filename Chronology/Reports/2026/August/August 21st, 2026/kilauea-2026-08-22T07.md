**Kīlauea Status – August 22, 2026**  

Kīlauea is not erupting; the summit eruption in Halemaʻumaʻu is paused. Data indicate the next fountering episode is likely between August 22 and 27. Deflation began last night, following prior deflationary patterns. Glow was observed from north and west vents overnight.  

Current Volcano Alert Level: ADVISORY  
Current Aviation Color Code: YELLOW  

WARNING: NWS forecasts a potential tropical storm system this weekend with torrential rain. Overlap with an eruption could create extreme conditions. Watch for closures. All of Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park, including HVO facilities, remains without power since Hurricane Lala.
