Summary:
 Kīlauea volcano is not erupting; the summit eruption in Halemaʻumaʻu is paused. Summit reinflation has increased over the past day. A forecast window of September 7-12 is likely for the next fountain episode.

Overview:

The summit eruption of Kīlauea in Halemaʻumaʻu is paused. A few very brief instances of flames were observed overnight from the north vent. Summit reinflation has been very irregular since lava fountaining episode 54 on August 25, and it has increased again over the past day. The current reinflation pattern is similar to what followed the last few episodes. A broad forecast window of September 7-12 is likely for the next episode if inflation follows the prior patterns.

No significant activity has been noted along Kīlauea’s East Rift Zone or Southwest Rift Zone.
