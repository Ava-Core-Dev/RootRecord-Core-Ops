Kīlauea Update

Current Status: Paused Eruption

The summit eruption of Kīlauea in Halemaʻumaʻu is currently paused. A deflationary step has been observed since yesterday morning, and a forecast window of September 7-13 is likely for the next fountain episode. 

Hazard Note: Gas emissions from Kīlauea are a concern for air quality in the surrounding areas.
