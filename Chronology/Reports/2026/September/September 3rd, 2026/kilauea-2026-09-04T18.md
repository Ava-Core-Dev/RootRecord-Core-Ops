# Kīlauea

Hazard Notification System (HANS) for Volcanoes

 Kīlauea volcano is not erupting; the summit eruption in Halemaʻumaʻu is paused. The summit is currently in a deflationary step.

Hazard Note: Deflation and inflation of the summit crater may cause sudden changes in ground deformation, potentially leading to landslides and other hazards.
